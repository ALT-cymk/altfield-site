The problem

One product with a blank SEO title is easy to fix by hand. A thousand of them, with meta descriptions and image alt text as well, is not.

Nothing is written until you approve it

Every proposal sits beside the product it belongs to. Tick what you want, leave the rest. Anything the model is unsure of is flagged and not pre-selected.

Generate only the fields you choose

Pick the fields that matter to you. Unticked fields are never sent to the model, so a narrower run is a cheaper one. Product names and descriptions stay off unless you ask for them — shoppers read those.

Undo a whole run in one click

Every change records its previous value before it is replaced. Reverting restores those exact values, makes no AI calls, and keeps working on the free plan.

New products fill themselves in

Switch on autopilot and products are filled in as you add them, so a catalogue stays complete instead of drifting back out of date. Off by default, chosen per field, and it only ever fills fields that are empty.

What Altfield will not do

The things that usually go wrong with apps like this

✓ Overwrite what you wrote

It fills gaps. Replacing a value you wrote yourself is a separate choice you make on a run.

✓ Take your work when you leave

Everything Altfield wrote stays in your store if you downgrade, cancel or uninstall. It is your data.

✓ Touch your customers' data

It asks for product and file access only. No customer scopes, no order scopes, nothing to leak.

What leaves your store

Written from your own product data

To generate a proposal, Altfield sends a product's own text to Anthropic's API — title, description, type, vendor, tags, existing SEO fields and existing metafields. Where a description is too thin to work from, it also sends one product photograph.

  • No customer, order or payment data is sent, because the app never has any.
  • Anthropic does not use data submitted through its API to train its models.
  • Data is held in the European Union, in Amsterdam.
