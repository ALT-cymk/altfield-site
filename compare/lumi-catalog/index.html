Altfield vs Lumi Catalog

Two apps doing the same job in almost the same way. The real difference is how the bill is counted — and there are things Lumi does that Altfield does not do at all.

The short version

Same workflow, different meter

Lumi and Altfield both scan a catalogue, draft the missing fields, show them to you, and write only what you approve. Anyone telling you one of them is the safe one and the other is reckless has not used both. What differs is the unit you are charged in, and how much else each app does.

Altfield charges per product, counted once per run however many fields that run writes. Lumi charges in credits: one credit for an alt text, an SEO title or a meta description, two for a product description, with categories and metafields counted once per product. Neither is a trick. They suit different shapes of work.

The arithmetic

Lumi's own worked example, priced both ways

Lumi publishes this one: 250 products, all four copy fields, three images each. By their worksheet that is 1,750 credits.

  • On Lumi's Maintain at $19/month and 500 credits, 1,750 credits is about four months, or one $79 Cleanup used inside its 90 days
  • On Altfield's Starter at $19/month, 250 products is one run inside a 750-product allowance

That is the honest shape of it: the more fields you want per product, the better Altfield's meter reads. The fewer, the better Lumi's does. A thousand alt texts and nothing else is 1,000 credits on Lumi: two months of Maintain, or a third of one $79 Cleanup. On Altfield the same job is 1,000 products, which is a $49 month rather than a $19 one.

Choosing

Where each one is the wrong answer

Pick Lumi if

You want to see your gaps before granting any app access to your store; you need drafts in a language other than the one your products are written in, or in a defined brand voice; you want store-page accessibility scanning as well as product fields; or the job is finite and you would rather pay $79 once than hold a subscription.

Pick Altfield if

You do not want to do credit arithmetic before you start; you want several fields on each product rather than one field on many; you want every feature on every plan, including the free one; or you want an undo that costs nothing, needs no AI call, and still works after you have stopped paying.
