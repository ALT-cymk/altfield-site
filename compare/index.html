The three options

Flat allowance, per-field credits, or automatic publishing

Most catalogue SEO apps fall into one of three models. They differ less in what they write than in what they charge for and what they do without asking you.

Questions

The ones merchants actually ask first

Do I even need an app for this?

If your catalogue is small enough to fix in an afternoon, no — open each product and type the fields in. The case for an app starts when the work is a thousand products wide and keeps coming back every time you add stock.

Can I not just use ChatGPT or Claude with a template?

You can, and the writing will be fine. The work is everything around it: exporting the catalogue, keeping the rows lined up, pasting the answers back, and having no record of what the old values were. Altfield does the same generation inside your Shopify admin, writes through the API so nothing lands in the wrong row, and keeps the previous value of every field so you can put it all back.

Will it overwrite the product copy I wrote myself?

Not unless you ask it to. A normal run only fills fields that are empty. Replacing existing values is a separate option you turn on for that run, and even then nothing is written until you tick it.

What happens to the text if I cancel?

It stays. Everything Altfield wrote is written into your own Shopify product records, so downgrading, cancelling or uninstalling takes nothing away.

How does a product count against my allowance?

Once per run, no matter how many fields that run writes for it. Running a product again next month counts again.

Are there fields it will not write on its own?

Product names and descriptions are off unless you switch them on, and autopilot will never write them at all — those are what a shopper reads, so a person looks at them first.

Where is my data processed?

Product text goes to Anthropic's API to generate a proposal; no customer, order or payment data is sent, because the app never asks for access to any. Altfield's own data is held in the European Union, in Amsterdam. Full privacy policy.
