Altfield vs TinySEO

TinySEO is a whole SEO and speed toolkit with thousands of reviews behind it. Altfield does one of the things it does. Here is the honest case for each.

The short version

A toolkit against a single tool

TinySEO, formerly TinyIMG, sells image compression, lazy loading, script control, CSS and JavaScript minification, redirects, sitemaps, schema, site audits and AI metadata generation in one app. Its site says 60,000+ merchants, 2,000+ reviews and 5/5 stars. Altfield does none of the speed or image work. It fills missing product metadata, and that is the whole app.

So the question is not which is better. It is whether you want one app covering a broad surface, or a narrow one that counts the metadata work in products rather than in AI credits.

The arithmetic

Where the meters diverge

Take 500 products, each needing an SEO title, a meta description and alt text on three images: five drafted fields per product, 2,500 in all.

  • On TinySEO that work is metered in AI credits, and the $14 Beginner plan includes 150 a month
  • On Altfield it is 500 products, inside Starter's 750-a-month allowance at $19

The reverse is just as true. If what you actually need is 8,000 images compressed and your metadata is already written, Altfield has nothing to sell you and TinySEO has the whole answer for $23.

Choosing

Where each one is the wrong answer

Pick TinySEO if

You want one app for images, speed and SEO rather than several; you want a long review history and tens of thousands of other merchants before you install anything; image compression is the real job; or you would rather an app got on with it than asked you to approve a list.

Pick Altfield if

The metadata is the job and you want it counted in products rather than credits; you want to read every proposed value before it is written; you want an undo that restores the exact previous text rather than a backup of your images; or you want the app to hold no customer or order data at all, with the records kept in the EU.

Where Altfield loses outright

On proof. TinySEO has thousands of reviews and Altfield has none, because it is new. That is a real reason to be cautious and we are not going to argue you out of it — install it on the free plan, run 25 products, and see.
